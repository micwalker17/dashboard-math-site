Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026. Dashboard Math is provided by Tallyfield Apps.

What the app reads

With your permission (the "read boards" scope), the widget reads the boards connected to it: board names, column names, groups, and item and subitem values. It reads them inside your browser, through monday.com, so it can calculate the figure you set up.

What the app stores

The widget saves its own settings (title, the numbers you defined, your formula and display options) in monday.com's app storage, inside your monday.com account. It does not store your board data, and we keep no database of our own.

What leaves monday.com

Nothing. Dashboard Math has no server of its own. Your board data and settings are not sent to us or to any third party, and we cannot see them.

Third parties

The app's files are hosted on monday.com's infrastructure. The app uses no other third-party services, domains, analytics or advertising.

Cookies and tracking

The widget sets no cookies of its own and contains no trackers.

Billing

Subscriptions are handled by monday.com. We receive from monday.com the account and subscription details it shares with app developers, such as account ID, plan and renewal status. We do not receive payment card details.

Support

If you email us for help, we keep your message and email address for as long as needed to answer and to keep a record of the request.

Deleting data

Removing the widget or uninstalling the app removes its saved settings from your account under monday.com's own retention rules. We hold no other copy.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new date.
