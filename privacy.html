Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026. Dashboard Math is provided by Tallyfield Apps.

What the app reads

With your permission (the "read boards" scope), the widget reads the boards connected to it: board names, column names, groups, and item and subitem values. It reads them inside your browser, through monday.com, so it can calculate the figure you set up.

What the app stores

The widget saves its own settings (title, the numbers you defined, your formula and display options) in monday.com's app storage, inside your monday.com account. It does not store your board data, and we keep no database of our own.

What leaves monday.com

Nothing. Dashboard Math has no server of its own. Your board data and settings are not sent to us, and we cannot see them. The only optional exception is the "Draft with AI" button described below, which talks to monday.com's own AI.

Draft with AI (optional)

If you choose "Draft with AI", the widget sends what you typed, the names and types of the board's columns, and a few example values from status-like columns to monday.com's own AI service (monday AI), using your account's monday AI allowance and the "consume AI" permission. The request goes from your browser to monday.com, not to us. Item names and number values are not sent. The AI only proposes settings, and nothing is saved until you press Save. If you never press the button, nothing is sent.

Third parties

The app's files are hosted on monday.com's infrastructure. Apart from monday.com, including monday AI when you use "Draft with AI", the app uses no other third-party services, domains, analytics or advertising.

Cookies and tracking

The widget sets no cookies of its own and contains no trackers.

Billing

Subscriptions are handled by monday.com. We receive from monday.com the account and subscription details it shares with app developers, such as account ID, plan and renewal status. We do not receive payment card details.

Support

If you contact us for help, we keep your message and contact details for as long as needed to answer and to keep a record of the request.

Deleting data

Removing the widget or uninstalling the app removes its saved settings from your account under monday.com's own retention rules. We hold no other copy.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new date.
