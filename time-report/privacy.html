Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026. Time Report is provided by Tallyfield Apps.

What the app reads

With your permission, the widget reads the boards connected to it ("read boards"): board names, group names, item and subitem names, and the time tracking columns with their session logs (start, end and who started each session). It reads the names of the people in those logs ("read users") so it can label rows. It reads all of this inside your browser, through monday.com.

What the app stores

The widget saves its own view settings (rows, period, range and display choices) in monday.com's app storage, inside your account. It does not store your board data, and we keep no database of our own.

What leaves monday.com

Nothing. Time Report has no server of its own. Your board data is not sent to us, and we cannot see it. The only optional exception is the Summarize button described below, which talks to monday.com's own AI.

Summarize (optional)

If you press Summarize, the widget sends the table on screen (row labels such as people's names or item names, and hours per period) to monday.com's own AI service (monday AI), using your account's monday AI allowance and the "consume AI" permission. The request goes from your browser to monday.com, not to us. The summary is shown on screen and not saved. If you never press the button, nothing is sent.

Third parties

The app's files are hosted on monday.com's infrastructure. Apart from monday.com, including monday AI when you press Summarize, the app uses no other third-party services, analytics or advertising.

Cookies and tracking

The widget sets no cookies of its own and contains no trackers.

Billing

Subscriptions are handled by monday.com. We receive from monday.com the account and subscription details it shares with app developers, such as account ID, plan and renewal status. We do not receive payment card details.

Support

If you contact us for help, we keep your message and contact details for as long as needed to answer and to keep a record of the request.

Deleting data

Removing the widget or uninstalling the app removes its saved settings under monday.com's own retention rules. We hold no other copy.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page with a new date.
