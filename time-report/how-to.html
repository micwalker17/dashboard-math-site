Time Report by Tallyfield Apps

How to use Time Report

Before you start

  • You need a monday.com board with a Time tracking column, on items, subitems or both. Hours come from timer sessions and from sessions added by hand in the column's log.
  • Members and admins can use the widget. monday.com does not let viewers load app data.

Install

  1. Open the monday.com apps marketplace and search for "Time Report".
  2. Choose Install and pick the workspaces that may use it.

Add the report to a dashboard

  1. Open a dashboard and choose Add widget, then Apps, then Time Report.
  2. Connect one or more boards that have a Time tracking column.
  3. The table appears right away, by person and week for the last four weeks.

Change the view

  • Rows: person, item, item and subitem, or group.
  • By: day, week or month.
  • Range: this week, last 4 weeks, this month, last 30 days, last 3 months or this year.
  • Include: items and subitems, items only, or subitems only.
  • Column: if the board has more than one time tracking column, pick one or use all.

Your choices are saved with the widget, so everyone who opens the dashboard sees the same view.

Summarize

Press Summarize for a short written summary of the table on screen. This uses monday AI on your account. If your account has no monday AI credits left, the button says so and the report keeps working.

Export

Export CSV downloads the table with hours as decimals, ready for a spreadsheet or invoice.

Good to know

  • Each session counts on the day it started. A session that crosses the start or end of the range counts only the part inside it.
  • Hours typed straight into a time column without a session log have no date or person, so they are left out. The widget shows how much time that is.
  • Very large boards are read up to 5,000 items, and the widget says so when that limit is reached.
